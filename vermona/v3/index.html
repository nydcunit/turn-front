Morning and evening, press 3–4 drops into clean, dry skin, then moisturise. Every morning, finish with broad-spectrum SPF 30+. Patch test 24 hours before first use. Mild tingling in week one is normal; stop if irritation continues.

  • Adults with melasma, sun spots, post-inflammatory marks or uneven tone
  • Fitzpatrick types I–VI
  • Sensitive skin, with a gradual start

Pregnant, breastfeeding, or have a spot that is changing or bleeding? Please see a doctor before use.
