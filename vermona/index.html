Why spots linger

Dark spots form in layers.
Vermona works on every one.

Sun exposure, breakouts and hormones trigger excess melanin that settles into the skin as stubborn patches. Single-ingredient products only address one step. Vermona works on several at once: it slows new pigment, blocks its transfer to the surface, and speeds up the turnover of the cells already discoloured.
