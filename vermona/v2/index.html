The science

Pigment forms in four stages. Vermona addresses each one.

Most brightening products act on a single step. Vermona combines actives with complementary mechanisms so excess melanin is reduced at the signal, the source, the transfer and the surface.

1

Signal

UV, heat and inflammation tell melanocytes to produce more pigment.

Tranexamic acidLicorice root
2

Production

The enzyme tyrosinase converts tyrosine into melanin.

Alpha arbutinAzelaic acid
3

Transfer

Melanin is packaged and passed up to surface skin cells.

Niacinamide
4

Surface

Pigmented cells sit at the surface and show as visible spots.

Azelaic acidVitamin C (THD)
