Results

Most people notice brighter, more radiant skin within about 2 weeks. Dark spots and marks typically start looking softer around week 4, with more visible improvement at 8–12 weeks. Deeper or long-standing pigmentation such as melasma can take longer. Consistent twice-daily use and daily SPF make the biggest difference.

Vermona is formulated for sun spots and age spots, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (marks left after acne or irritation), melasma-prone skin and general uneven tone. It is designed to fade discolouration, not to change your natural skin tone.

Faded spots can stay faded, but new pigmentation can form with sun exposure, hormones or breakouts. Daily sunscreen and continued use (or a maintenance routine once you're happy) help keep skin even.

Yes. Our actives (tranexamic acid, niacinamide, azelaic acid and alpha arbutin) are well suited to deeper skin tones, which are more prone to post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation. The formula avoids harsh ingredients that can cause irritation-induced darkening.

Ingredients & Safety

How to Use

Morning and night, apply 3–4 drops to clean, dry skin and press into the face and neck. Follow with moisturiser. Every morning, finish with broad-spectrum SPF 30+.

Yes, Vermona layers well with most routines. If you use retinoids or exfoliating acids (AHA/BHA), start by alternating nights to avoid over-exfoliation, then build up as your skin tolerates it.

One 30 ml bottle lasts about 8 weeks with twice-daily use. We recommend a 16-week course (2 bottles) for best results on stubborn spots.

UV exposure is the biggest trigger of new pigmentation. Without daily SPF, new spots can form faster than old ones fade. Sunscreen is essential for seeing results with any brightening product.
