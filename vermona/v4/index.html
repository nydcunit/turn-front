Advanced anti-pigmentation

Even skin begins here.

Vermona Anti-Pigmentation Serum lightens skin and targets hyperpigmentation, melasma, sun damage and darkened areas on the face and body. Choose your course length. Your first course is 20% off at checkout.

Not sure which course?

  • Hyperpigmentation and dark spots
  • Melasma
  • Sun damage, sun spots and age spots
  • Post-acne marks
  • Darkened underarms, knees, elbows and inner thighs
  • Dull, uneven tone

Morning and evening, apply 3–4 drops to clean, dry skin and follow with moisturiser. Use SPF 30+ every morning on treated areas exposed to sun. Patch test each new area 24 hours before first use.

Aqua (Water), Azelaic Acid, Niacinamide, Propanediol, Tranexamic Acid, Alpha-Arbutin, Glycerin, Glycyrrhiza Glabra (Licorice) Root Extract, Tetrahexyldecyl Ascorbate, Sodium Hyaluronate, Panthenol, Allantoin, Xanthan Gum, Hydroxyethylcellulose, Sodium Phytate, Ethylhexylglycerin, Phenoxyethanol.

  • Weeks 1–2: brighter, more even-looking skin
  • Weeks 4–8: dark spots and marks visibly lighter
  • Weeks 12–16: melasma and stubborn areas improve
