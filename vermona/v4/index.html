The formula

Five actives. Every strength disclosed.

Each one targets a different stage of how dark pigment forms, so skin lightens faster without harsh bleaching agents.

Signal3%

Tranexamic Acid

Calms the triggers behind melasma and new dark patches.

Production2%

Alpha Arbutin

Slows the enzyme that makes melanin, for fewer new spots.

Transfer5%

Niacinamide

Stops pigment reaching the surface and strengthens skin.

Surface10%

Azelaic Acid

Fades existing marks and calms redness and breakouts.

Protect1%

Licorice + Vitamin C

Antioxidants that brighten and defend against daily UV.
